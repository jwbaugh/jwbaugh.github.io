My group develops models of software, to make it more robust and reliable—and models of engineering and scientific systems, to improve the way we design and analyze artifacts in the real world.

Areas we draw from include

formal methods: mathematical techniques for specifying, developing, and verifying software systems

systems science: computational approaches from operations research to help make better decisions

Applications in engineering include coastal hazard mitigation, civil site development, vehicle routing and scheduling, air quality management, structural optimization and design, and active control systems.

Our projects touch on many different facets of problem solving, from theoretical aspects and formal models to the practical issues of building complete systems that incorporate state-of-the-art technology. Representative projects include Alloy case studies, subdomain modeling for large-scale storm surge simulation, and Vitri parallel optimizer.
